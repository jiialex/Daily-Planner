# AI Planner – Smart Daily Planning and Productivity Assistant

**AI Planner** is an intelligent productivity application designed to help users organize their daily activities, manage tasks, plan projects, and achieve their goals more efficiently. By using artificial intelligence, the system helps users create personalized schedules, prioritize important tasks, set reminders, and manage their time effectively.

The application provides a user-friendly dashboard where users can view their daily tasks, upcoming deadlines, calendar events, and progress toward their goals. It can analyze tasks based on priority, deadlines, and estimated completion time to recommend a practical daily schedule.

AI Planner aims to reduce stress caused by poor time management, improve productivity, and help users maintain a better balance between work, education, and personal activities.

### Key Features
- **AI-powered scheduling:** Generate personalized daily and weekly plans.
- **Task management:** Create, edit, organize, and track tasks.
- **Smart prioritization:** Identify urgent and important activities.
- **Calendar integration:** Organize appointments, events, and deadlines.
- **Reminders and notifications:** Help users remember important activities.
- **Progress tracking:** Monitor completed tasks and productivity.
- **Goal management:** Break large goals into smaller, achievable tasks.
- **Personalized recommendations:** Suggest ways to improve time management based on user preferences and activity patterns.

### Main Objective
The main objective of AI Planner is to provide an intelligent, easy-to-use platform that helps users plan their time, organize their responsibilities, and accomplish their daily and long-term goals through AI-assisted decision-making.

### Target Users
Students, university instructors, employees, entrepreneurs, freelancers, and anyone who wants to improve their organization and productivity.

### Vision
To make everyday planning smarter, simpler, and more effective by combining task management with artificial intelligence.
